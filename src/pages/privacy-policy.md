*Last updated: September 20, 2026.*

Seasoned Steel ("we", "this site") is a static website. We keep data collection to a minimum.

**What we collect**
- **Advertising:** We use Google AdSense, which uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- **Affiliate links:** Links to Amazon use Amazon Associates tracking. Amazon's privacy practices are governed by [Amazon's Privacy Notice](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=468496).
- **Analytics:** If enabled, we use privacy-respecting, cookieless analytics (e.g., Plausible) to understand aggregate traffic. No personal data is collected.
- **Hosting:** This site is hosted on Vercel, which may process standard server logs (IP address, user agent) for delivery and security.

**What we never do**
We do not sell, trade, or rent personal data. We do not run email newsletters unless explicitly stated, and we never share contact details.

**Your rights**
Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA, and equivalents), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data held about you. Contact us via the [contact page](/contact/).

**Changes**
If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
